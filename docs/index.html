Keep your macOS audio devices where you put them.

Take out an AirPod, walk near your iPhone, or plug in a monitor — and macOS silently switches your mic or speakers mid-call. Audio IO Keeper pins your chosen input and output and moves them straight back the moment macOS drifts.

How it works

Audio IO Keeper lives quietly in your menu bar with no Dock icon or background clutter. Every action it takes is visible in its popover activity log.

One-click device pinning

Click any output or input device (or its pin) in the menu-bar popover to switch to it immediately and lock it in blue. Click the blue pin again to unlock.

Settings lock pickers & alert sounds

Choose or unpin locked devices directly in Settings → Locked devices, and optionally lock macOS system sound effects to your pinned output.

Smart duplicate labels

Dual monitors or HDMI + DisplayPort endpoints with identical names are automatically disambiguated by connection type (and a short UID tag when needed).

Graceful disconnects & wake

If a pinned device unplugs, macOS uses its fallback without errors and your pin is restored as soon as the device returns — including slow-waking docks and displays.

Anti-thrash conflict backoff

If another app or automation repeatedly overrides a default, the keeper backs off with growing cooldowns (2 s to 60 s) and surfaces the conflict instead of fighting.

15-minute temporary unlock

Need AirPods for a single call? Unlock pins for 15 minutes from Settings — your pins stay remembered, survive relaunch, and lock back automatically when time is up.

What’s new in v1.3.1

Focused reliability, responsiveness, and multi-display polish across macOS 14+.

  • Reliable menu-bar device list & clicks: Fixed a popover layout collapse when devices populate asynchronously and switched device/pin controls to native AppKit-backed borderless buttons for immediate first-click selection.
  • Per-device Core Audio hot-plug listeners: Tracks stream, liveness, name, and source transitions per device — catching slow-waking DisplayPort/HDMI monitors, docks, and Bluetooth headsets automatically (plus coreaudiod restarts).
  • Duplicate device disambiguation: Automatically labels colliding device names with their port (e.g. HDMI vs DisplayPort).
  • Interactive Settings & alert-sound locking: Pick or unpin Output and Input directly in Settings, and optionally lock system alert sounds to your pinned output.
  • Post-wake follow-up & Universal DMG: Reconciles immediately on wake plus a 1.5 s follow-up scan, packaged as a Universal (arm64 + x86_64) Release disk image.

Safe by construction

Audio IO Keeper only reads and writes the standard Core Audio default-device properties — the exact same settings that System Settings → Sound uses. Quitting the app leaves your Mac exactly as it was.

Install

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. On first launch, Audio IO Keeper adopts your current input and output as initial pins so protection starts immediately.

  1. Download AudioIOKeeper-1.3.1.dmg from GitHub Releases (or build it yourself with scripts/make-dmg.sh).
  2. Open the disk image, drag AudioIOKeeper onto Applications, and eject the DMG.
  3. Launch Audio IO Keeper from Applications. Look for the waveform icon in your menu bar.
  4. First launch only: release builds are ad-hoc signed (not notarized by Apple yet). Right-click the app in Applications → Open → Open, or open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway after the first blocked attempt.
